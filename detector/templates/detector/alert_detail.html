How these explanations are calculated

Feature attribution. Each of the transaction's own features is swapped for its value in 12 typical legitimate training transactions, the GNN rescores it against the same graph neighbourhood, and the fall in log-odds is averaged. One-hot columns such as country are swapped as a whole. When features overlap (the behavioural risk score summarises the other behavioural flags, for example), they share the credit, so read them together.

Graph evidence. The GNN rescores the transaction with one entity hidden at a time, so it can't use that account's, device's or IP's history.

Counterfactual. Drivers are swapped cumulatively in order of importance until the GNN score falls below its alert threshold. This shows what the decision hinged on; it isn't a claim about what a real legitimate transaction would look like.
