Charts

Hover a chart for exact values. Click an entry in any legend to hide or show it on every chart. Each chart has a data table underneath.

ROC curves

Share of frauds caught against the share of legitimate transactions flagged. The axis is logarithmic because fraud detection happens below 1% false positives. Dots mark the alert thresholds.

Precision–recall curves

With so few frauds this is the fairer view: how many alerts are real frauds (precision) as more frauds are caught (recall). Dots mark the alert thresholds.

Frauds caught at the alert threshold

Out of {{ res.test_frauds }} frauds in the test period.

False alarms

Legitimate transactions the GNN would have flagged.

Score distributions

Where the GNN puts frauds (red bars) and legitimate transactions (grey outline), as a share of each group. The further apart they sit, the cleaner the separation. The vertical line is the alert threshold.

  • Frauds
  • Legitimate
  • Alert threshold

Training curves

Validation PR-AUC as the GNN learned, every 5 epochs. Training stops once it no longer improves, and the best point (dot) is the epoch that was kept.

Transaction graph

The whole graph the GNN learns from. Every transaction is linked to the account, card, device, IP and merchant it used. Accounts sit in the middle of their own cluster, with their cards, devices and IPs around them. Clusters spanning several accounts share a device or IP, and when they carry fraud they're listed as fraud rings. Merchants link almost everything, so they sit on the outer ring.
