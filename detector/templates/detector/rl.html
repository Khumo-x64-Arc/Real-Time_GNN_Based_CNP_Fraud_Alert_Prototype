RL agent

A Deep Q-Network on top of the semi-supervised GNN, after FraudGNN-RL (Cui et al., 2025) and DRL+GNN (Almasan et al., 2022). Only a fraction of transactions ever get a label, and late. The GNN learns from those plus pseudo-labels, and the agent decides the alert threshold, how much to trust labels propagated through the graph, and when to pseudo-label, one time window at a time.

How it works

  1. Graph. European Cardholder: each transaction links to up to 5 earlier transactions from the past hour with cosine similarity above 0.9 (FraudGNN-RL's construction). IEEE-CIS: links through account, card and device.
  2. Semi-supervised GNN. Trained on the revealed labels only. Then self-training: unlabelled training transactions the GNN is surest about become half-weight pseudo-labels for another round. The round with the best labelled-validation PR-AUC is kept.
  3. The agent's world. The validation and test periods are cut into time windows. In each one the agent sees a readout of the GNN's embeddings plus feedback so far, and picks a threshold, a label-propagation weight and a pseudo-labelling confidence (63 actions).
  4. Reward. Caught minus missed frauds minus 0.2 per false alarm, counted on labelled transactions only. Labels arrive a few windows late.
  5. Comparison. On the test stream, against a fixed threshold, the best single fixed setting, and the agent without the graph.
